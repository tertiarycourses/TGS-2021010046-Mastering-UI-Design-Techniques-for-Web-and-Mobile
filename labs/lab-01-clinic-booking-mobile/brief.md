# Lab 01: Clinic Booking Mobile

**Client:** CityCare

A Singapore clinic needs a mobile flow for appointment search, booking and rescheduling. Patients include older adults with 200% text settings.

**Deliverables:** Mobile prototype with search, appointment details, error and confirmation states.

**Acceptance:** 390 px mobile; appointment completion <= 45 seconds; large text and clear recovery.

**Assessment mapping:** A1 A2 A3 A4 A5.
