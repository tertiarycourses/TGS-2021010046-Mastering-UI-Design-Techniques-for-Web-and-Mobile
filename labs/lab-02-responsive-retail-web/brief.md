# Lab 02: Responsive Retail Web

**Client:** TrailGoods

A local outdoor retailer wants a product discovery and cart flow for desktop and mobile. Stock and delivery promises must be explicit.

**Deliverables:** Responsive home, listing, product and cart screens.

**Acceptance:** 1440/768/390 px; card and button variants; keyboard focus.

**Assessment mapping:** A1 A2 A3 A4 A5.
