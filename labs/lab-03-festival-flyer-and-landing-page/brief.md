# Lab 03: Festival Flyer and Landing Page

**Client:** Neighbourhood Arts Week

A community arts festival needs an A5 print flyer and matching mobile landing page for free workshops.

**Deliverables:** A5 flyer, QR destination landing page and print proof.

**Acceptance:** 3 mm bleed; 5 mm safe zone; matching event details.

**Assessment mapping:** A3 A4 A5 A6.
