# Lab 04: Programme Brochure

**Client:** Harbour Learning

An adult-learning provider needs a tri-fold brochure for a short course with fees, schedule and registration details.

**Deliverables:** Six-panel tri-fold brochure and accessible PDF review copy.

**Acceptance:** Correct panel order; readable type; factual schedule and contact.

**Assessment mapping:** A3 A4 A5 A6 A7.
